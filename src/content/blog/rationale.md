This year has brought us the [Vulnpocalypse](https://matthiasott.com/notes/at-machine-speed). This fall a slew of zero days in PHP and even things like Ruby. The best way forward is to just reduce the attack surface as good as we can. For public-facing webpages, this means in my opinion to just go back to static site hosting from one of the big providers. This raises the question: how do we replace WordPress and still empower your average Joe to make changes to his hompage?

AstraeusCMS tries to bridge that gap. Try out the [backend.](https://astraeuscms.build/admin/) And all is being dog-fed on a mobile phone in the gym. In my defence there's like one other guy here.
